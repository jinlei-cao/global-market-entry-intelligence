## Citation notes

- Use the **actual** source access date and download manifest from the run when reporting observed statistics.
- The WGI series underwent methodology revisions and updates. Use the current-vintage publication when finalising a research paper, and keep version information.
- The historical survey LPI 2023 and LPI 2.0 measure related but distinct constructs. No direct conversion is assumed in this project.
- An example of software citation is available in the root `CITATION.cff`. All research results require independent reproduction.
